what does God actually command of you
1. The foundation — love. Jesus was asked which command was greatest, and He answered directly: "Love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, with all your mind, and with all your strength," and "love your neighbor as yourself." He said "there is no commandment greater than these" and that "all the Law and the Prophets" hang on them (Mark 12:29-31; Matthew 22:37-40). Every other command below is an outworking of this, not a separate list competing with it.

2. The moral core — the Ten Commandments still apply to you.

Have no other god before Him; don't worship anything in His place.
Don't make or bow to idols.
Don't misuse God's name.
Keep a rhythm of rest and worship (most Christians apply this to honoring the Lord's Day, Sunday, rather than the literal seventh-day Sabbath, though Christians genuinely differ on the details here).
Honor your father and mother.
Do not murder.
Do not commit adultery.
Do not steal.
Do not lie or bear false witness.
Do not covet what belongs to someone else.

Repent and believe — turn from sin, trust in Christ (Mark 1:15; Acts 2:38). You've already begun doing exactly this.
Be baptized as a public declaration of that faith (Matthew 28:19; Acts 2:38).
Put off specific sins Paul names directly — sexual immorality, impurity, greed, anger, malice, slander, lying (Colossians 3:5-9; Galatians 5:19-21).
Put on specific character — "compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness, patience... forgiving each other" (Colossians 3:12-13); the fruit of the Spirit: "love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control" (Galatians 5:22-23).
Be honest, generous, and just — don't steal, work honestly, give to those in need (Ephesians 4:28), care for the poor and oppressed (echoing the same heart we saw in Leviticus 19).
Forgive others as you've been forgiven (Matthew 6:14-15; Colossians 3:13) — this one matters a lot, since you're asking for forgiveness yourself right now.
Pray continually (1 Thessalonians 5:17), and don't neglect gathering with other believers (Hebrews 10:24-25).
Guard sexual purity specifically — the New Testament is direct and repeated about this (1 Corinthians 6:18-20; 1 Thessalonians 4:3-5).
Love even your enemies
